# Portuguese Golf Handicap Rules Summary (2024)
## Regras de Handicap - Em vigor desde janeiro de 2024

---

## 1. OVERVIEW & PRINCIPLES

The World Handicap System (WHS) is administered in Portugal by the Federação Portuguesa de Golfe (FPG) in compliance with USGA and R&A guidelines. The system aims to:
- Allow golfers to obtain and maintain a handicap index
- Enable golfers of all abilities to compete fairly on any golf course worldwide
- Ensure the handicap accurately reflects demonstrated golfing ability
- Update handicaps promptly and consistently

---

## 2. HANDICAP INDEX CALCULATION

### 2.1 Score Differential Calculation

**For 18-hole rounds:**
```
Score Differential = (113 ÷ Slope Rating) × (Adjusted Gross Score - Course Rating - PCC)
```
- Rounded to 1 decimal place (0.05 rounded up)

**For 9-hole rounds (converted to 18-hole equivalent):**
```
9-hole Score Differential = (113 ÷ Slope Rating of 9 holes) × (Adjusted Gross Score of 9 holes - Course Rating of 9 holes - (0.5 × PCC))
```

**Important notes:**
- Negative score differentials are rounded UP toward zero (e.g., -1.4 → -1.4; -1.5 → -1.5; -1.6 → -1.6)
- PCC (Playing Conditions Calculation) has a range of -1.0 to +3.0
- Scores must meet minimum course rating thresholds
- Stableford and Par/Bogey scores are converted to point values for differential calculation

### 2.2 Handicap Index Calculation (From Score Differentials)

**With fewer than 20 score differentials:**

Use a lookup table based on the number of differentials in the record. The lowest score differentials are averaged with an adjustment factor:

| # of Differentials | # Used in Calculation | Adjustment |
|-------------------|----------------------|------------|
| 1 | 1 (lowest) | -2.0 |
| 2 | 1 (lowest) | -2.0 |
| 3 | 1 (lowest) | -2.0 |
| 4 | 1 (lowest) | -1.0 |
| 5 | 1 (lowest) | 0 |
| 6 | 2 (lowest) | -1.0 |
| 7-8 | 2 (lowest) | 0 |
| 9-11 | 3 (lowest) | 0 |
| 12-14 | 4 (lowest) | 0 |
| 15-16 | 5 (lowest) | 0 |
| 17-18 | 6 (lowest) | 0 |
| 19-20 | 8 (lowest) | 0 |

**With 20 or more score differentials:**

```
Handicap Index = (Average of 8 lowest Score Differentials - Difference from Low Index) + Limiter adjustments
```

Where:
- Average of 8 lowest recent score differentials (including any extraordinary results adjustments)
- Difference between this average and the Low Handicap Index:
  - If > 3.0, apply soft cap (50% of amount > 3.0)
  - If > 5.0 after soft cap, apply hard cap (limiter máximo)

### 2.3 Handicap Index Plus (+)

When a player's Handicap Index is negative (better than scratch), it is designated as "Plus" (e.g., -2.5 becomes "+2.5"). The "+" must always precede the handicap in all applications.

### 2.4 Maximum Handicap Index

Maximum Handicap Index: **54.0**

### 2.5 Low Handicap Index (Soft Cap)

When a player's calculated Handicap Index is lower than their Low Handicap Index by more than 3.0 strokes, a soft cap is applied:
- Calculate difference from Low Handicap Index
- Apply 50% reduction to amounts > 3.0
- Increases are limited to 50% of the calculated increase

### 2.6 Hard Cap (Limiter Máximo)

After soft cap is applied:
- Maximum increase of 5.0 strokes above Low Handicap Index
- Cannot descend below established boundaries
- No downward limit exists

## 3. PLAYING HANDICAP & COURSE HANDICAP

### 3.1 Course Handicap (18 holes)

```
Course Handicap = Handicap Index × (Slope Rating ÷ 113) + (Course Rating - Par)
```

Rounded to nearest integer (0.5 rounded up).

**When playing same 9 holes:**
```
Course Handicap = Handicap Index × (Slope Rating of 9 ÷ 113) + (2 × Course Rating of 9 - 2 × Par of 9)
```

### 3.2 Playing Handicap (for competitions)

```
Playing Handicap = Course Handicap × Handicap Percentage
```

Rounded to nearest integer (0.5 rounded up).

**Handicap Percentages by Format:**
- Generally applied at 85-100% for level competition
- May vary by competition type (stroke play, match play, scramble, etc.)
- Details in Appendix C

### 3.3 9-hole vs 18-hole Conversions

For 9-hole competitions with multiple tees:
- Play both front and back 9 on different days: Create two 18-hole score differentials
- Duplicate the 9-hole result with same Course Rating and Slope Rating
- Calculate 18-hole equivalent Course Handicap
- Apply 50% of PCC adjustment to the 9-hole round

## 4. SCORE SUBMISSION & REQUIREMENTS

### 4.1 Eligible Score Formats

Players must submit scores in **authorized formats**:

**18-hole formats:**
- Organized Competition
- General Play
- Par/Bogey - Competition
- Par/Bogey - General Play
- Maximum Score - Competition
- Maximum Score - General Play

**9-hole formats:**
- Organized Competition
- General Play
- Par/Bogey - Competition
- Par/Bogey - General Play
- Stableford - Competition
- Stableford - General Play

**Additional:**
- Four-Ball (under specific conditions - Rule 5.10)

### 4.2 Minimum Holes & Score Validity

**For 18-hole results:**
- Minimum 10 holes completed for score acceptance
- 9-18 hole results must be played in authorized format
- Course Rating and Slope Rating must be in effect

**For 9-hole results:**
- Minimum 9 holes completed
- Must have Course Rating and Slope Rating for 9 holes
- Converted and counted as 18-hole equivalent

### 4.3 Result Submission Timeline

- Submit same day as round completion, before midnight (local time)
- If not submitted on play day:
  - Handicap Index NOT updated next day
  - Result NOT included in PCC calculation
  - Result may be submitted out of order
  - Must be registered in chronological order in handicap record

- When result submitted after previous round played:
  - PCC recalculated for day when first round was played
  - Handicap Index recalculated

### 4.4 Score Adjustment Rules (Rule 3)

**Maximum Hole Score (Net Double Bogey):**

Before Handicap Index is established:
- Maximum per hole = Par + 5 strokes

After Handicap Index established:
- Maximum per hole = **Duplo Bogey Net** (Double Bogey + 2 + any Handicap strokes on hole)
  - Or minimum: Stableford zero points

**When a hole is not played:**
- Uses expected result (resultado esperado)
- Based on Handicap Index and field difficulty
- Combines expected and actual results = 18-hole differential

## 5. PLAYING CONDITIONS CALCULATION (PCC)

### 5.1 Definition

Statistical daily adjustment comparing players' actual results against standard expectations on a given day.

**Objective:** Recognize when actual conditions differed from normal, creating need for compensation.

### 5.2 PCC Calculation Process

Performed once daily for each course:

1. Calculate expected result for each eligible player
2. Calculate standard deviation (σ) of score differentials at the course
3. Determine number of scores above/below expected
4. Calculate PCC adjustment based on difficulty
5. Applied as integer to all players' differentials at that course on that day

**Range:** -1.0 to +3.0

**Applied when:**
- At least 8 acceptable scores submitted
- Score of 36.0 or less (Handicap Index ≤ 36.0)
- No reduction if fewer than 8 acceptable scores

### 5.3 Adjustments Based on Conditions

- If fewer players better than expected → easier conditions → PCC reduces differentials (negative)
- If more players better than expected → harder conditions → PCC increases differentials (positive)

---

## 6. HANDICAP INDEX ADJUSTMENT & FREQUENCY

### 6.1 Revision Frequency

Handicap Index must be updated by end of day following score submission, or as soon as possible thereafter.

**Recommended:** Update same day if feasible.

**Timing for competitions:**
- If new round played before Handicap Index updated (including multiple rounds same day), use existing Index
- Committee may determine when Index updates occur

### 6.2 Handicap Index Minor (5-year lookback)

Lowest Handicap Index achieved in preceding 365 days, serving as reference point for upward movement restrictions.

- Reevaluated when new Handicap Index submitted and visible in record
- Can change more than 365 days ago if relevant
- Soft cap/hard cap applied relative to this index

---

## 7. EXTRAORDINARY SCORES & ADJUSTMENTS

### 7.1 Extraordinary Score Reduction

When a Score Differential is 7.0+ strokes **better than** current Handicap Index:

**Reduction applied:**
| Score Differential Difference | Reduction |
|------|-----------|
| 7.0 - 9.9 strokes better | -1.0 |
| 10.0+ strokes better | -2.0 |

**Notes:**
- Adjustment applied when recalculating Handicap Index
- Based on difference between unadjusted differential and current Index
- Multiple reductions for multiple extraordinary scores cumulative
- Reduction permanent until 20 newer differentials submitted
- With fewer than 20 differentials, applied to all registered

---

## 8. SCORE ADJUSTMENTS (Adjusted Gross Score)

### 8.1 Maximum Per Hole - Before Handicap Established

**Par + 5 strokes** maximum per hole

### 8.2 Duplo Bogey Net (After Handicap Established)

```
Maximum = Par + 2 + any handicap strokes received on hole
```

Or minimum of Stableford zero points.

**Applied automatically** when:
- Using hole-by-hole record (preferred method)
- Submitted by player as adjusted gross score

**Application:**
- When handicap index > 54 and receives 4+ strokes on hole → max becomes Par + 5
- Handicap strokes integer-only applied; fractional strokes not permitted

---

## 9. ACCEPTABLE SCORE FORMATS & FORMS

### 9.1 Authorized Formats

**Stroke Play:**
- Gross scores, net scores, maximum score formats

**Par/Bogey:**
- Converted to point values using Stableford scoring scale

**Stableford:**
- Direct point scoring (converted to strokes for differential calc)

**9-hole Rounds:**
- Duplicated with 9-hole Course Rating and Slope Rating
- Converted to 18-hole equivalent

**Four-Ball (new):**
- Individual score of player in Four-Ball competition (18 holes) eligible if:
  - Score meets standard requirements
  - Individual performs 6+ strokes better than par (42+ Stableford points)
  - Calculated per Stableford procedure

## 10. PLAYING CONDITIONS CALCULATION IN DETAIL

### 10.1 Factors Affecting PCC

Course conditions (e.g., rough, pin positions, green firmness)
Meteorological conditions (wind, temperature, moisture)
Course configuration (hole routing, layout changes)

### 10.2 PCC NOT Recalculated When

- Variation extreme during two distinct parts of day
- Player composition significantly different in competition vs general play
- Multiple rounds played same day at same course

### 10.3 PCC Differentiation

May apply different PCC for different competition when:
- Only players in that competition included
- Only that group's differentials applied to other general play players at that course

## 11. COMMITTEE RESPONSIBILITIES (Rule 7)

### 11.1 Handicap Committee Functions

**Procedural Revision:**
- Execute annual minimum revision (all members designated on club record)
- May conduct additional reviews at any time
- Strongly recommended for all clubs

**Adjustment Authority:**
- Reinitialization using 20 recent differentials with adjustment per table
- Retraction to selected level for period (requires Federation approval)
- Reduction by 1.0 per adjustment rule
- Suspension (for persistent rules violations)
- Reinstatement after suspension period

**Before making adjustments, examine:**
- Potential impact of injury, illness, prolonged absence
- Handicap history and demonstration of ability
- Any evidence of objective non-compliance with rules
- Whether adjustment justified or already achieved naturally

### 11.2 Competitive Committee Responsibilities

**Technical Committee** responsible for competition determines:
- Maximum Handicap Index for entry
- Maximum Playing Handicap for competition
- Adjustment of Playing Handicap when Field evidence insufficient
- Maintains Competition Handicaps during event

**Optional:** May maintain Competition Handicaps unchanged during tournament if multiple rounds played.

---

## 12. SPECIAL RULES

### 12.1 Inactive Players

Handicap Index ONLY dropped if member leaves club. Otherwise maintained in record.

### 12.2 Handicap Suspension

Committee/Federation may suspend when player:
- Deliberately or recurrently violates rules
- Refuses to comply or acknowledge responsibilities
- Fails to submit results timely without justification

**Suspension requirements:**
- Player informed and opportunity to respond
- Notice of duration provided
- Additional conditions may apply

### 12.3 Handicap Reinstatement

After suspension, Committee determines level by:
- Restoring prior valid level
- Assigning new from scratch status
- Using last registered Handicap Index

---

## 13. COURSE RATING & SLOPE RATING (Appendix G)

### 13.1 Definitions

**Course Rating:** Difficulty for scratch golfer under normal conditions
**Slope Rating:** Difficulty for bogey golfer relative to course rating

### 13.2 Minimum Requirements

Effective 2024:
- **18-hole course:** Must be ≥ 1370 meters
- **9-hole course:** Must be ≥ 685 meters

Allows results acceptance at courses meeting minimum length for Course Rating and Slope Rating.

### 13.3 Multiple Tee Considerations

When competition uses different tees with different pars, may require:
- Additional handicap strokes calculation
- Difference in par between tees
- Proper allocation per competition format

---

## 14. NINE-HOLE SCORING RULES

### 14.1 9-hole Conversion Methodology

When playing 9-hole tournament from different tees:
- Result duplicated using same 9-hole Course Rating and Slope Rating
- Creates 18-hole equivalent Score Differential
- Applied 50% of PCC to 9-hole round only
- Remaining 50% applied to 18-hole combination

### 14.2 9-hole Results with Multiple Tees

Scoring registered as 18 separate combinations when:
- Front/back 9 each played
- Different tees with different pars
- Each combination receives individual Score Differential

### 14.3 Club with Multiple 9-hole Courses

When club has three 9-hole courses:
- PCC calculated for any 18-hole combination (South/East, South/West, East/West, etc.)
- Result duplicated for all 18-hole combinations
- Properly weighted in Score Differential calculation

## 16. KEY FORMULAS SUMMARY

### Score Differential (18-hole)
```
SD = (113 ÷ SR) × (AGS - CR - PCC)
```

### Score Differential (9-hole)
```
SD = (113 ÷ SR9) × (AGS9 - CR9 - (0.5 × PCC))
```

### Course Handicap (18-hole)
```
CH = HI × (SR ÷ 113) + (CR - Par)
```

### Course Handicap (9-hole)
```
CH = (HI ÷ 2) × (SR9 ÷ 113) + (CR9 - Par9)
```

### Playing Handicap
```
PH = CH × Handicap%
```

### Duplo Bogey Net (DHN)
```
DHN = Par + 2 + Handicap Strokes on Hole
```

## 18. HANDICAP REVISION PROCESS (Detailed)

### 18.1 Annual Minimum Revision

Committee must execute annual review for all members with designated clubs.

**Procedure:**
1. Examine all evidence (20 recent results, ability demonstration)
2. Identify if current Index reflects demonstrated ability
3. Consider fitness, injury, absence, significant performance change
4. Reinitialization using 20 most recent differentials with adjustment if necessary
5. Document decision with supporting evidence

### 18.2 Exceptional Adjustment Authorization

Requires Federação Autorizada approval when increasing Index more than 5.0 strokes.

### 18.3 Committee Discretion

May apply adjustments downward to any level, considering:
- Demonstrated exceptional performance vs current Index
- Previous ability levels and history
- Any objective evidence supporting change

---

## 19. INACTIVE PLAYER STATUS

Handicap Index maintained indefinitely in record unless player leaves club.

No automatic deactivation or removal.

Return from inactive status requires no special process.
